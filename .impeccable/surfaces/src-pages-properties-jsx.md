Scope: home / listings page (src/pages/Properties.jsx), plus the shared header and footer (src/pages/Layout.jsx) and listing card (src/components/PropertyCard.jsx). Mode: Persuade. Stage 1 of a staged redesign; the detail page, admin and UX fixes follow.

Audience and job: Hebrew-first visitors, mostly on mobile, looking for premium property; the primary action is searching (city, deal type, property type) and opening a listing.

Constraints: owner-pinned direction (editorial luxury). No invented stats or numbers. Keep every feature and the Exclusive badge. Code-led build (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A real listing's photograph is the first viewport, not a blue gradient. This page refuses the SaaS hero with stat tiles. The brokerage speaks through one decisive photo, a Hebrew serif headline and a search that works where it sits.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ink (#1b1916) and limestone (#f3efe8) grounds, with brass (#9a7440, darker #7a5a2b for text) as the only accent. Frank Ruhl Libre for headlines, Heebo for text and numerals. Square-ish 4px corners, hairline stone rules, no card boxes and no blue.

STORY: Visitors see a real property at full scale, understand this is a curated Israeli brokerage, filter in place, and open a listing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed photo of the featured exclusive listing with an ink scrim from the start side. A large white serif H1 with the subtitle beneath. A search panel sits at the bottom of the hero: three selects plus a primary "Show N listings" button carrying the live count. A small caption plate at the bottom-end corner names the photographed listing and links to it.

FORM: pinned by the user (editorial luxury), not rolled; no seed key.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
